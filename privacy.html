Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Contact: dev@memrya.co

MEMRYA is a conversation-memory service in limited testing. This policy describes what data MEMRYA processes, how service providers are involved, and what happens when you connect your Google Calendar. If you have questions or want to exercise your privacy rights, contact dev@memrya.co.

Data we collect and create

Depending on the features you use, MEMRYA handles account identifiers and profile details, recordings and imported audio, transcripts and language metadata, speaker labels, saved memories, summaries, searches, user feedback, and information needed to provide the service. Recording audio may include other people's voices and sensitive information: obtain any legally required permission before recording or uploading a conversation.

Audio, transcription and AI processing

MEMRYA records audio on your device and can send selected recordings to cloud processors to transcribe, analyze, and help you recall conversations. Supabase provides account and cloud data services. AssemblyAI and/or Speechmatics can receive audio for transcription, depending on the route. OpenAI can receive transcripts, excerpts and search context needed for analysis and memory features. If you opt into voice identity features, pyannote.ai may receive limited speech samples to help match speakers across recordings. Voice identity may involve biometric information and requires appropriate consent.

What Google Calendar access does

Connecting your Google Calendar is optional. MEMRYA asks Google for permission to manage calendar events on calendars you own. Google's permission screen may describe read, change and delete capabilities because those are included in the Google scope. MEMRYA's current Calendar workflow does not scan your existing calendar in the background. It suggests a calendar event from the conversation analysis, displays its title, date and time for review, and sends the event to Google only after you explicitly confirm creation. If details are incomplete, you can correct them before approval.

For an approved event, Google receives the event details you reviewed, such as title, start/end time, time zone, and any location or description you chose to include. MEMRYA stores Google OAuth access and refresh tokens encrypted in private, account-specific server storage so it can act on your behalf; tokens are not exposed to other MEMRYA users. OAuth state and pending event drafts are temporary. No additional guests are invited by the current flow.

MEMRYA does not sell Google user data or use Google Calendar information for advertising or to train general-purpose AI models. Google Calendar account data is used only to provide the Calendar connection you request. Conversation recordings and transcripts are processed separately according to the audio/AI processing section above, not imported from Google Calendar.

Other service providers and payments

Service providers receive only the information relevant to their role. Apple and RevenueCat handle applicable purchase and subscription information, which may be linked to an internal account identifier. We do not sell recordings or Google Calendar data to advertisers.

Storage, safeguards and retention

MEMRYA uses private cloud storage and account-based access controls. Data and credentials are protected through server-side mechanisms, but MEMRYA does not claim end-to-end encryption. The amount of time information is retained depends on the data category, the feature and applicable processing-provider rules. Contact us for details regarding an individual recording, voice profile, or account. We do not promise immediate deletion from third-party systems beyond their applicable retention rules.

Your choices and requests

Connecting Google Calendar and enrolling voice identity are optional. You can disconnect Google Calendar from the MEMRYA Connections screen; this deletes MEMRYA's stored connection and attempts revocation at Google. You may also revoke MEMRYA from Google Account permissions. Disconnecting does not remove events already created in your calendar. To request access, correction, export or deletion of account information, use available in-app controls or the account-deletion instructions, or email dev@memrya.co.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features change. The current dated version will remain available on this page. Email dev@memrya.co for privacy and security questions.
