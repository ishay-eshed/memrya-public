How MEMRYA handles your recordings, transcripts and account data.

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

Contact: dev@memrya.co

This page explains how MEMRYA handles recordings, transcripts and account data, including cloud processing and account-level access controls.

What MEMRYA processes

MEMRYA's core flow is recording or importing a conversation, then uploading, transcribing and analyzing it to produce a summary, evidence and grounded answers you can search later. Audio you record or import is the basis for everything else the product creates.

Where processing happens

Audio is recorded on your device. MEMRYA asks for cloud AI processing consent during setup. With that consent, saved recordings and voice Quick Memories may be processed automatically.

Providers that receive your data

MEMRYA uses Supabase for account services and cloud storage. Account-linked recordings, transcripts, memories and related records can be stored there.

When you process audio, AssemblyAI and/or Speechmatics receive audio for language detection and transcription, according to the processing route.

OpenAI receives transcript text and related details for analysis. Where AI-assisted memory search is available and you allow it, OpenAI can also receive your search and relevant conversation excerpts. Searches answered entirely on your phone do not send data to OpenAI.

RevenueCat receives an account-related user ID and purchase information to validate purchases, manage subscription access and provide subscription analytics.

Connected services

When you authorize a supported Zoom or Google connection, MEMRYA may receive recordings or transcripts under the permissions you approve. Google Calendar is covered separately in the section “Google user data (Google Calendar)” below: MEMRYA does not read your calendar. A Google Calendar draft can be saved without direct calendar access. You can revoke provider permissions in the provider account.

Google user data (Google Calendar)

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. MEMRYA does not access your Google account or calendar unless you connect it. This section applies only to information MEMRYA receives from Google APIs when you do. It is separate from your MEMRYA conversation content (recordings, transcripts and the summaries and tasks MEMRYA creates from them), which is handled as described in the rest of this page.

MEMRYA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy.

What MEMRYA asks Google for

One permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned. Google presents this as a permission over events on calendars you own, which is broader than what MEMRYA does with it. MEMRYA requests no other Google permission.

What MEMRYA does with it

MEMRYA uses the permission only to add one event to your primary Google Calendar after you ask it to. MEMRYA first shows you the exact event: title, date, start and end time or all day, and any notes. Nothing is added until you explicitly confirm. The event is created as private, with no guests and no video-call link. The title and any notes come from your MEMRYA conversation content, such as a task with a due date or a meeting you asked to add; MEMRYA never invites anyone.

MEMRYA does not list, read, search, change or delete any other event, does not access your other calendars and does not sync your calendar. Its only read is a lookup of the same event it just created, used to avoid creating a duplicate when a request is retried.

What MEMRYA stores

  • The access and refresh tokens Google issues, and the permission you granted. They are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on MEMRYA's servers, are never sent to the app, and are used only to create the events you confirm.
  • A short-lived snapshot of the event you are about to confirm, valid for 10 minutes, so that exactly what you reviewed is what is created.

MEMRYA does not copy your existing calendar events. Events it creates live in your Google Calendar, where you manage them.

How it is used and shared

MEMRYA uses Google user data only to provide the add-to-calendar feature you requested. It does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. It does not send Google user data to the AI or transcription providers listed on this page. MEMRYA personnel do not read Google user data, except with your affirmative agreement, as needed for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or to comply with applicable law.

Disconnecting, revoking and deletion

You can disconnect Google Calendar at any time in the app's Connections screen. MEMRYA deletes the stored authorization first, then asks Google to revoke it. If Google's revocation cannot be confirmed, the app tells you to also remove MEMRYA in your Google Account under third-party access, which you can do at any time. Events already added stay in your calendar until you delete them in Google Calendar.

To have your Google authorization and related data removed, disconnect Google Calendar first, then use the Delete account page or contact dev@memrya.co. To ask about your Google data, contact dev@memrya.co.

Your processing choices

Cloud AI processing requires your consent. Without it, core processing is unavailable. To stop future processing or request deletion, contact support or use available account controls. This does not automatically erase information already sent to providers.

Storage and access

Recordings, transcripts and related account data are stored in private cloud storage. Text Quick Memories and organizational data can also sync to your MEMRYA account. Account permissions limit access to the content associated with your account.

Secrets and keys

Provider and API secrets used to talk to processing providers live server-side. They are never embedded in the mobile client.

Encryption

We do not claim end-to-end encryption. Data is protected through private, owner-scoped cloud storage and server-side secret handling as described above, not through an end-to-end encryption guarantee.

Languages

Spoken language and result language are handled separately in the product. Processing providers support a broad set of languages, but transcription quality is not equal across every one of them.

Optional voice identity

Where Voice Identity is available and you choose to enroll, MEMRYA can send a speech sample from an existing conversation to pyannote.ai to create and match a reusable voice profile. This profile contains biometric voice information used to suggest speaker matches across recordings. Enrollment requires explicit consent, including permission from the person whose voice is used. Matches can be incorrect and may need your confirmation.

Voice profiles are distinct from recordings and transcripts. To ask about the storage, processing, retention or removal of voice identity data, contact dev@memrya.co. Available controls may vary by app version. This description does not mean Voice Identity is available to every account.

Retention

Cloud retention and deletion follow MEMRYA controls and the applicable rules of the processing providers involved. Contact us if you have a question about a specific recording or account.

Export and deletion

To request an account export or deletion, use the Delete account page or contact dev@memrya.co.

No analytics or trackers

This website uses no analytics, cookies, trackers or third-party JavaScript, and sets no public API keys.

Questions

For any privacy question, contact dev@memrya.co.
