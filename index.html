Privacy and control

  • Private to your account

    Your memories and recordings are stored privately under your account, with access limited to that account.

  • Processed only when you ask

    You decide when recording starts. Cloud transcription and analysis begin only after you choose to process a conversation.

  • Answers you can check

    Answers link back to the conversation and the moment they came from, so you can verify them yourself.

  • Your data, your choice

    You can request a copy of your data or delete your account. Service credentials are kept on our servers, never inside the app.

Common questions

What does MEMRYA actually record and store?

MEMRYA turns a recorded or imported conversation into a transcript, summary, decisions, people and evidence you can search later. Cloud storage is private and access is owner-scoped.

How much will MEMRYA cost?

MEMRYA Pro is listed at US$19.90 per month with 900 processing minutes. Extra minute packs are available. Final local pricing and renewal details are confirmed in the App Store before purchase.

Can I import from Zoom or Google Meet?

Not yet. Zoom cloud recording import is in development, Google Meet is planned next and Microsoft Teams later. You can already import audio files you have today.

Is my data encrypted end-to-end?

No. MEMRYA does not offer end-to-end encryption. Your data is kept in private cloud storage with access limited to your account, and each account's data is kept separate.

How long is my data retained?

See the Privacy page for current information about storage, retention and deletion controls.

Can I delete my account and data?

Use the Delete account page or contact dev@memrya.co to request export or deletion of your account and data.
