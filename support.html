Support

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Contact: dev@memrya.co

Contact our support team at dev@memrya.co. MEMRYA is currently in limited testing.

Google Calendar connection

Each subscriber connects their own Google account through Settings → Connections → Google Calendar. Google asks for permission before MEMRYA can create events. Confirm the account shown by Google is the one whose calendar you want to use. After connecting, MEMRYA proposes calendar events for review at the end of analyzed conversations; nothing is added until you approve.

If Google shows that the app is still in testing, only accounts permitted in the Google testing configuration can authorize it. This limitation will be removed when the app is approved for public access. For permission or connection problems, email the approximate time and the error message, but never send passwords, authorization codes, OAuth tokens or API keys.

Recording and privacy

MEMRYA processes recorded conversations through external transcription and AI services. Check Privacy for details on data handling, voice recognition, Google Calendar access and your choices.

Account deletion and subscription help

For account export/deletion requests, see Account deletion or email support. For App Store billing, review your subscriptions in Apple Account settings. For unusual charges or a failed processing job, send a description without sensitive recordings unless requested through a secure channel.
