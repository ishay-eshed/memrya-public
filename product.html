01 / CAPTURE

Remember the conversation, not the note-taking

Record on your iPhone or import existing audio. Once you choose to process it, MEMRYA extracts the transcript and structured details such as topics, decisions, actions and source evidence.

  • Audio import and iPhone recording
  • Automatic spoken-language detection where supported
  • Speaker segmentation and contextual naming when evidence supports it

02 / REMEMBER

One evolving memory. Many conversations

Related decisions, commitments, tasks and open questions can surface together across your account. The original conversation remains available as evidence; MEMRYA does not erase where an insight came from.

Unlike a stack of transcripts, the view is organized around what continues to matter over time.

03 / MEMORY SEARCH

Find answers across the conversations that matter

Use Memory search to find open tasks, commitments, decisions or a topic you discussed before. It looks across your connected memory and the original conversations to put together an answer based on what was said.

Answers should point back to their underlying source, so you can check context rather than accept an untraceable claim.

Understand Memory search and sources →

“What did we agree about the event?”

“Which tasks are still open?”

“What did we discuss with this person?”

“Which questions did we leave unresolved?”

04 / ACT

Let the important things come back

See actions, reminders and unfinished threads from conversations. Mark tasks done and avoid repeatedly resurfacing completed work. Notification preferences are designed to respect quiet hours and lock-screen privacy.

Reminder availability depends on your app version and notification permissions.
