Remember the promises that matter at home

02 / FRIENDS

Keep the plan, even when it changes

A weekend idea turns into a plan, and the meeting point changes again. Save a conversation when everyone agrees, or make a Quick Memory afterward. MEMRYA is designed to bring those saved details together when you need them.

Quick Memory means you add the detail yourself; this does not imply automatic messaging-app access.

03 / DOCTOR

Remember what you wanted to ask next

With appropriate permission, a recorded April appointment can preserve the clinician's instructions and your open questions. After a May follow-up, the saved conversations can help you review which questions were answered and what still needs confirming.

MEMRYA is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a licensed professional. Always confirm important health information with your clinician.

05 / MEETINGS & CALLS

Take the next conversation where the last one ended

A planning meeting sets a delivery date. A later call changes it. At the next check-in, Memory search can help you retrieve the latest decision and who agreed to follow up, using saved conversation sources.

Record only where permitted, or import an existing supported audio file. MEMRYA does not automatically record ordinary phone calls.
