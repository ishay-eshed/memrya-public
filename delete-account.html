Delete account

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Contact: dev@memrya.co

To request account deletion or an export of your personal data, use the available MEMRYA account controls or email dev@memrya.co from the email address associated with your account. We may need to verify that you own the account before acting.

What a deletion request covers

A deletion request can include your account profile, stored conversations, transcripts, memories, voice identity data and associated cloud records where supported. Some information may need to be retained for lawful obligations, purchase records, fraud prevention or service security, and third-party processing services may have independent retention schedules. We will explain material exceptions when handling a request.

Connected Google accounts

Disconnect Google Calendar from MEMRYA first, if possible. Account deletion should remove the stored calendar connection credentials; it does not delete events already saved in your Google Calendar. You can separately revoke access in your Google Account permissions.

Subscriptions

Deleting your MEMRYA account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple. Cancel recurring billing in your Apple Account subscriptions settings before deleting the account.

Contact

Email dev@memrya.co. Never email passwords, one-time login codes or raw authentication credentials.
