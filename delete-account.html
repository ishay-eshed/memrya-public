Account and data requests

In supported versions of MEMRYA, open Settings to export your data or delete your account. If you cannot access those controls, email support from the address associated with your account so we can verify ownership.

For account security, email requests should come from the address associated with your MEMRYA account.

How to delete your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account, then review the confirmation. If you cannot use the app, email dev@memrya.co from your account address to request deletion.

What happens next

In-app deletion requires confirmation. If you contact support, we may request additional information to verify ownership before handling your request.

Data export

In supported app versions, select Export data in Settings before deleting your account. If that control is unavailable, request a data export by email before deletion.

Deleting your MEMRYA account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel it separately in your Apple account subscription settings to stop future renewals. Deleting the app also does not cancel the subscription.
